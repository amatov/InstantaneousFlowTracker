MATLAB files implementing the mixture-fitting algorithm described in M. Figueiredo and A. K. Jain, "Unsupervised learning of finite mixture models" (IEEE, 2002), and in N. Ueda and R. Nakano, "Deterministic annealing EM algorithm" (Neural Networks, 1998).

The wrap-around clustering was implemented by Alexandre Matov and extended to circular clustering by Shayan Modiri.
